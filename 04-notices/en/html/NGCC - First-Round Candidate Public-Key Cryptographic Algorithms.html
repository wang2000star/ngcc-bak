NGCC: First-Round Candidate Public-Key Cryptographic Algorithms

2026-09-20
Font size:  Big   Middle   Small

In accordance with the Submission Requirements for Public-Key Cryptographic Algorithms, the Institute of Commercial Cryptography Standards (ICCS) has conducted the procedural review of the received public-key cryptographic algorithm proposals, and hereby announces the list of 84 candidates that will be moving on to the first-round evaluation. The first-round evaluation is expected to be completed by June 2027. All interested parties are welcome to make public comments of the algorithms through the NGCC forum.
